          📖 How to Download Your YouTube & Instagram Data in 2 Minutes

          1. YouTube Watch History (Google Takeout)
          1. Go to takeout.google.com and click "Deselect all".
          2. Scroll to the very bottom and check "YouTube and YouTube Music".
          3. Click "All YouTube data included" → click "Deselect all" → check only "history" → click OK.
          4. Create export, download the .tgz or .zip file, and drag it directly into this page!
          2. Instagram Watched Reels (Meta Accounts Center)
          1. Go to Instagram Accounts Center → Download your information.
          2. Choose "Some of your information".
          3. Scroll to the very bottom under Ads information and check "Ads and topics" (this is where Meta stores videos_watched.json and posts_viewed.json!).
          4. Optionally check "Likes" and "Saved" under Your Instagram activity.
          5. Select Format: JSON, download the .zip file, and drag it directly onto this page!
